# Approving a Batch is the Recruiter's only decision before calling

Agents propose people in Batches of about 20, each with a Why Now; the Recruiter drops the ones they don't want, optionally saying why, and approves the rest in one word, which finds their phone numbers and puts them on the Call List.
A human still decides who is called, but once per Batch rather than once per person, and phone numbers (the costliest data) are only bought for approved people.
Agents order people by judging their dated Signals, with no score or formula, because one Recruiter reading a Why Now needs no numbers; a formula comes back only if the agency product needs explainable ranking for the EU AI Act.

## Considered Options

- A decision per person (the old Reach Out): rejected as too slow for building a list worth an afternoon of calls.
- Agents filling the Call List on their own within a daily budget: rejected because nobody would decide who is called, and a bad Niche Profile would spend money silently.
