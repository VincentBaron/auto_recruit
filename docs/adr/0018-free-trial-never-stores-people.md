# The free trial shows Prospects in full but never stores people

A free Test Run shows Prospects with their Signals and Detected Roles in full, served from the company data shared across Workspaces, so it costs us almost nothing.
Hiring Contacts and Candidates appear only as Previews generated on the fly and never stored, because personal data cannot be cached across Workspaces (ADR-0004) and storing it would start the Article 14 one-month clock for users who may never pay.
Identities and contact details are sourced and stored for a Workspace only once it spends Credits: revealing the free Test Run's people on the first purchase is charged at the normal price per Hiring Contact and Candidate, and the checkout says so before payment.
Free Test Runs are limited to one per company domain and to work accounts, to stop the trial being used to harvest lists.
