# Limits on automated outreach

Email is the only channel our agents use to contact people.
LinkedIn and other platforms are never automated, because automating them breaks their terms of use and gets Recruiters' accounts banned; phone numbers are shown so Recruiters can call themselves.
In the app, our own agents never send a reply to someone who answered: they classify the reply and draft a response, and a Recruiter sends it, because a conversation in progress is where a wrong word costs a placement.
API callers can perform every action the app can, including sending replies, and the Workspace is accountable for what its integrations do.
