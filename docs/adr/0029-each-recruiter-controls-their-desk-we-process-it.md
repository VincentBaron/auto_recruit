# Each Recruiter is the controller of their Desk, and we are their processor

A Desk holds names, mobile numbers and call notes about people who never asked to be in it, so someone has to answer for them under the GDPR.
The Recruiter decides whom to look for and whom to call, so each Recruiter is the controller of their own Desk and we process it on their instructions, under a short data processing agreement signed when they accept their invite.
This is also what agencies will expect from the agency product, and it keeps us from answering for how each Recruiter calls people.

## Consequences

- A person who asks to be forgotten is erased from the Desk and added to the Do-Not-Call List, which keeps only what is needed to never call them again.
- Exa, the phone waterfall provider, Mistral (voice transcription) and Anthropic are listed as our sub-processors.
- Desks are hosted in France (ADR 0028).
- A Recap's voice note is deleted as soon as it is transcribed; only the text stays, in the Attempt.
- When a Recruiter stops, they get their Desk as markdown files, and it is deleted from the server and from its backup 30 days later, after two reminders.

## Considered Options

- We are the controller and Recruiters are our users: simpler paperwork, but it makes us answerable for every call a Recruiter decides to make.
