# Each Workspace is the data controller; we are its processor

Each Workspace decides whom to source and contact, so it is the GDPR controller for its Candidates, Hiring Contacts and outreach, and the Article 14 Notice names it as such.
We act as its processor under the data processing agreement in our terms, and we publish our sub-processors (hosting, model providers, Exa, enrichment providers, warmup service, payments).
We are controller only for the shared company data and for the hashed cross-Workspace Suppression List, which we keep on the legitimate interest of honouring objections.
