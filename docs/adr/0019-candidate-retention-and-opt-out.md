# Candidate retention periods and opt-out

Candidate data is kept only as long as a clear purpose justifies it, with periods simple enough to state in the Article 14 Notice.
A Surfaced Candidate who is never Contacted is deleted 25 days after being Surfaced, inside the one-month limit for informing people whose profiles are saved.
A Contacted Candidate who has not consented is deleted 90 days after the first email, and a reply without consent does not extend that period.
A Candidate who chooses the Talent Pool on the Consent Page is kept for 2 years from that choice.
An Opt-out, or the "delete my data" choice, deletes the Candidate's data immediately, because an objection ends the legitimate interest; only a hash stays on the Suppression List so they are never contacted again.
