# Scores are computed by a formula from LLM-extracted Signals

Ranking people for recruitment is a high-risk AI system under the EU AI Act (obligations apply from 2 December 2027) and an automated-processing concern under GDPR Article 22.
So the LLM only extracts discrete, dated, evidence-backed Signals and judges Fit, and a deterministic formula with per-Niche weights and age decay computes Signal Strength.
Every score can be explained line by line, tuned per Niche and compared across days, which an LLM-produced score cannot, and a human always decides who gets contacted.
